You acknowledge and agree that:

- this is a human wellness check-in, not an automated clinical service,

- it does not constitute a diagnosis, clinical assessment, or recommendation for medical treatment,
- and you may disable this feature at any time via Account → Privacy → Horizon Check-ins.

Any outreach from MBI in connection with Horizon is a supportive wellness contact only and carries no clinical authority.
